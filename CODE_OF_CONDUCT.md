# Code of conduct

This is a hobby modding project. Be decent.

* Be kind and patient. People arrive with very different experience of modding and of computers.
* Disagree with ideas, not with people. No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
* No piracy: do not ask for, offer or link to pirated games, cracks or game files. Do not post game content (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
* Respect other people's work: credit the teams whose tools we use ([CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md)) and the mod authors you mention.
* Keep discussion on topic, and keep private information (yours and others') out of public posts.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use GitHub's *Report content* on the comment, or the private security report form described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
